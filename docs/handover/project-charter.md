# SITE-V1 project charter

## Product

Create Andrés Atencio's central professional site and a reproducible Software
Development CV. Both must be immediately credible to a person involved in a
software-development hiring decision.

Base identity: **Software Developer · IT Teacher**.

The public narrative integrates a long software trajectory and current IT
teaching. Teaching explains the present; it does not erase development or turn
the site into a teaching-only profile.

## Audience

- recruiters;
- technical leads and engineering managers;
- founders;
- potential clients or collaborators.

They should quickly understand who Andrés is, that the software experience is
real, what kind of work he has done, what he does now, what distinguishes the
trajectory and how to contact him or open the CV/GitHub.

## Voice

Precise, sober, human, contemporary and technically competent. Prefer concrete
work over claims. Avoid ex-developer framing, fabricated seniority, AI Engineer,
rockstar/10x/wizard language and generic "passionate problem solver" copy.

## Product constraints

- Static Astro site with normal scrolling and strong semantic HTML.
- Site works completely without the decorative cable.
- The entire repository, including public site content and the Software
  Development CV, is English.
- Site and CV may use different copy for shared approved facts.
- No visitor-facing AI, backend, CMS, database or authentication in SITE-V1.
- No artificial portfolio projects added only for appearance.
- Public history contains only approved projection data and public-safe code,
  assets and documentation.

## Method

Work through reviewable increments:

`understand → decide → produce → review → verify → consolidate`

R1 establishes the complete static experience; R2 adds the cable interaction;
R3 hardens the final CV and public delivery. Releases are cumulative.

## Done

SITE-V1 is complete only when:

- the site is public and represents the trajectory accurately;
- the Software Development CV is complete and ready to send;
- site and CV are factually and narratively coherent;
- responsive, accessibility, performance and browser checks pass;
- the source/projection workflow can maintain both;
- Andrés has explicitly approved the final publication.
