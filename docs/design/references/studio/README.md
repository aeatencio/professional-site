# SITE-V1 studio visual references

These five images and the accompanying textual proposal are design-process
checkpoints from the SITE-V1 graphic direction work. They are kept here to
document that process and as visual inspiration. Text and images belong to the
same studio exploration.

They do not describe the current or final implementation. They have no
normative authority. Current product, design and content decisions live in
the repository's present instructions, accepted ADRs, contracts, code and
tests.

These files are not published assets. Do not serve them from the site, place
them under `public/`, or consume them from production code.

Together they formed one visual family and were used as checkpoints for a
later longitudinal page experience. They are historical references, not a
specification.

That exploration influenced visual-direction aspects that may still be present
on the site — for example asymmetric composition, page continuity,
illustrations as masses or objects, and spatial rhythm. The concrete cable
mechanism and its scroll behavior are not current product requirements.
